Userscripts for editing MusicBrainz in bulk.

Twelve scripts that live inside MusicBrainz's own pages: importing credits, fixing relationships by the hundred, chasing ISRCs, sorting cover art, merging duplicates. Each one works on its own; String Theory is all of them in a single file.
