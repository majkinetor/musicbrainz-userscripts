How weights, comments and export work

Choose a feature's importance: Ignore (0), Minor (1), Normal (3), Important (5) or Essential (10). Covered earns its full weight, Partial earns half, and Gap earns zero. The weighted verdict updates as you edit. Unknown coverage stays Needs assessment; an uncovered essential feature prevents a “Mostly replaceable” verdict. All ignored features mean Not applicable.

A score of 100% means Replaceable; 75% or more means Mostly replaceable unless an essential feature is uncovered; other positive scores mean Partial overlap. Zero covered weight means No equivalent. The AI's original verdict remains visible beside your weighted result.

Weights reflect your workflow. They do not increase the evidence's confidence. Use comments to explain corrections and gaps. “Post comment” adds a local, timestamped comment; it does not send anything to GitHub.

Changes save in this browser when storage is available. Export reviews for AI downloads JSON containing your weights, coverage corrections, comments, original findings and source/version references. Give that file to the AI with the report. Import restores it; newer edits win and posted comments are combined. Keep using this file path, or export/import when moving the report to a different browser or location.
